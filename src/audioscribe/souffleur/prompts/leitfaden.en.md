In addition, the moderator has prepared an agenda (section "Agenda"): items with IDs (P1, P2, ...) that are to be resolved in this meeting. The agenda is not knowledge. It is no evidence for anything, and deviating from it is not a contradiction.

Under "punkte" you report each item that a segment of the window substantively refers to:
- "angesprochen" (raised): the topic of the item is discussed but not conclusively answered or decided.
- "geklaert" (resolved): the item is clearly answered or decided in the window (an answer, responsibility, deadline or decision is stated and not questioned).
- "segment_id" and "aussage": the segment and a verbatim excerpt from it that shows this.
- "stand": for "angesprochen", what is still missing for the item; for "geklaert", the result in short form. At most 200 characters, in English.
- Report only items that really occur in the window. A merely similar keyword is not enough. When in doubt, report nothing. If there is no such item, "punkte" is empty.
- An agenda item on its own is not a finding. Report "befunde" as above, independently of the agenda.
