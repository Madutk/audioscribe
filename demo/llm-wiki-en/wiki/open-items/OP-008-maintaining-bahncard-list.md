# OP-008 · Maintaining the BahnCard list

## Question

How is the BahnCard Excel list kept up to date (new cards, expiry, people leaving)? Why is the BahnCard status still a required detail of the travel request when the Assistant has all of the team's BahnCards in the list?

## Why it is open

The Assistant keeps a list of all BahnCards on the team. [Q-002 · 00:01:12] At the same time, the traveler has to state in the request whether they have a BahnCard. [Q-001 · 00:00:34] ⚠️ inferred: The detail may serve to check or update the list; the sources say nothing about this.
