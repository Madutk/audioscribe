# Q-002 · Process recording 2: Fare, payment and filing

## Roles involved

- Moderator (records the process, not a domain role) [Q-002]
- [[ROL-001-assistant|Assistant]] (books the train trips for the team) [Q-002]

## Summary

The second recording adds fare, payment and filing to the train booking. The default fare is the Flex fare, because dates often shift; the Assistant uses the Saver fare only when the date is fixed and the trip is more than fourteen days away. Payment is made with the corporate credit card stored in the business customer account. From 250 euros per booking, the team lead has to approve by email beforehand; below that, the Assistant books directly. The Assistant keeps the team's BahnCards in an Excel list and enters the number when booking so the discount is applied. The invoice is downloaded as a PDF and filed on the team drive in the Travel folder by year and month. Accounting receives the invoices collected once a month, at the end of the month. Changes and special cases are announced for the next recording.
