# Q-003 · Process recording 3: Changes, cancellations and special cases

## Roles involved

- Moderator (records the process, not a domain role) [Q-003]
- [[ROL-001-assistant|Assistant]] (books the train trips for the team) [Q-003]

## Summary

The third recording covers what can happen after the booking. If a trip is called off, the traveler emails the Assistant, and the Assistant cancels; with the Flex fare this is free of charge until one day before the trip. If only the time changes, the Flex fare ticket stays unchanged because it is valid for any train that day; the Assistant only rebooks the seat reservation. Travelers handle delay refunds themselves. The Assistant does not book international trips; they go through the travel agency. The Assistant is covered by a person from the admin office, who also has access to the business customer account. Maintaining the BahnCard list when a card expires is not defined; the Assistant usually notices the expiry only when the discount no longer applies during booking. The Moderator records the unclear responsibility for the BahnCard list as an open item.
