# Q-001 · Process recording 1: Train booking process overview

## Roles involved

- Moderator (records the process, not a domain role) [Q-001]
- [[ROL-001-assistant|Assistant]] (books the train trips for the team) [Q-001]

## Summary

The recording gives an overview of how a train booking works from the Assistant's point of view. Every booking starts with a travel request sent by email to a team mailbox. The request must contain the travel date, origin, destination, latest arrival time and BahnCard status; the Assistant asks for missing details by email. Bookings are made in the browser on bahn.de using a business customer account. Second class is booked as a rule, first class only for trips over four hours, always with a seat reservation. The ticket is downloaded as a PDF and emailed to the traveler. The volume is about ten bookings a week, a lot more before trade fairs; with a complete request, one booking takes about ten minutes. Fare and payment were deferred to the next recording.
